# Form distribution

This section covers the ways forms can be delivered to end users for completion.

It includes:

* sharing forms by link
* sending forms to patients
* [embedding forms and packages](aidbox-ui-builder-alpha/embedding.md) in an application
* printing forms
* working with forms offline

[Form packages](form-packages.md) can be shared through one link or sent through the email workflow. Each link belongs to a package run; the recipient completes its enabled forms and submits them together. See [sharing a package](aidbox-ui-builder-alpha/form-sharing.md#sharing-a-package) and [sending a package](aidbox-ui-builder-alpha/form-sending.md#sending-a-package).
